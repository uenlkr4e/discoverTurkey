# Content plan: question pages

Short, specific pages that answer one question travellers search for, and link to the bigger destination pages and guides. Big sites own broad searches such as "Istanbul travel guide". These narrow questions are where a small site can rank.

## How to use this list

- **Check demand before writing.** These are educated guesses, not measured search volumes. Before you write a page, check the question in Google: look at the "People also ask" box and related searches, and at whether small sites already rank. Once the site has been live for a few weeks, **Google Search Console → Performance** shows the searches you already appear for. Pages ranking in positions 8–30 are the best ones to improve or expand.
- **One question per page.** Answer it in the first two sentences, then give the detail. Use the question, or something close to it, as the title.
- **Link both ways.** Each question page links to its hub (the "Hub" column), and the hub links back to it, under **Related** or in the text.
- **Add first-hand detail.** What you saw, how long it took, what you'd do differently. This is what makes a page worth ranking, and it's what AI-generated summaries can't copy.
- **Follow the accuracy policy.** No fixed prices, opening hours or fares. Link to the official source, and list time-sensitive claims under **Facts to verify**.

Priority: **A**: write first (clear demand, little good competition, fits existing content). **B**: next. **C**: seasonal or niche; publish ahead of the season.

## Suggested new guide categories

The current categories cover transport, money, connectivity, etiquette, safety and seasons. Two more would fit this plan:

- **Planning**: "where to stay", "is it worth it" and "how many days" questions.
- **Outdoors**: hiking, diving, skiing and national parks.

Add them in `src/data/guide-categories.yaml` (or in the CMS) before writing pages for them.
