  **Antalya Airport (AYT)** is one of Turkey's busiest, with year-round domestic flights and many seasonal international routes. Airport buses, the tram and taxis connect it with the city.

  The tram and city buses cover the centre, Konyaaltı and the bus station, using a rechargeable transport card. Kaleiçi itself is pedestrian-friendly, with steep lanes down to the harbour.

  The ancient sites outside the city are easiest to reach by hire car or organised tour. Some local buses run towards Perge, Aspendos and Side.

Antalya is the largest city on Turkey's Mediterranean coast and the gateway to the "Turkish Riviera". It sits on a cliff-lined bay with the Taurus Mountains rising behind it. Many visitors know it only for the large beach resorts along the coast to the east and west, but the city has much more to offer: a characterful old town, a world-class museum and several remarkable Roman cities within an hour or two's drive.

## Old town or beach?

**Kaleiçi**, the old town, is the most atmospheric place to stay, with small hotels in restored houses and everything within walking distance. It is hilly and has few beach options right on the doorstep. **Konyaaltı** to the west and **Lara** to the east are better if a beach is the priority, and are popular with families.

## Ancient sites near Antalya

The region was the ancient land of Pamphylia, and its cities were rich in the Roman period:

- **Perge**: colonnaded streets, baths and a stadium in a flat, open site east of the airport.
- **Aspendos**: one of the best-preserved Roman theatres in the world.
- **Side**: an ancient port town with a seaside Temple of Apollo, now also a busy resort.
- **Termessos**: a mountain city never conquered by Alexander the Great, in a national park.

Combining Perge and Aspendos in one day is easy by car. Start early in summer: there is little shade at either site.

## With children

Antalya works well for families, with long beaches, boat trips and a mild climate for much of the year. Midday summer heat is intense, so plan outdoor activities for the morning and late afternoon.
