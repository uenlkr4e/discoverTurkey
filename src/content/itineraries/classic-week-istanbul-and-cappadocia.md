This is the classic first-time route through Turkey: three full days in Istanbul for its Byzantine and Ottoman monuments, then a short flight to Cappadocia for a complete change of scene, among volcanic valleys, rock-cut churches and some of the most memorable sunrises anywhere.

## Before you go

- **Book the flight between Istanbul and Cappadocia early.** Check which Istanbul airport it leaves from, because the two are far apart.
- **Plan for balloon cancellations.** Flights depend on the weather. Book for your first morning in Cappadocia so you have spare mornings if it's cancelled.
- **Pack for both places.** Bring modest clothing and a scarf for mosque visits, and sturdy shoes for the valley trails. Cappadocia is often cooler than Istanbul, especially at night.

## Adapting the plan

If you have more time, add a fourth day in Istanbul for Dolmabahçe Palace, Balat and Fener, or a Bosphorus cruise. To add the coast, fly from Kayseri or Nevşehir towards Antalya or İzmir and continue from there.
