Turkey holds an extraordinary density of history: Hittites, Greeks, Romans, Byzantines, Seljuks and Ottomans all left their mark. This ten-day route connects three of the richest areas for visitors: imperial Istanbul, the Greco-Roman cities of the Aegean, and the Byzantine rock churches of Cappadocia.

## Before you go

- **Pick your season.** Spring and autumn are by far the best: the Aegean sites are hot, exposed and crowded in high summer.
- **Check the domestic flights.** Routes between İzmir and Cappadocia vary by season. If there's no direct flight, connect through Istanbul or Ankara.
- **Consider a museum pass.** If you plan to visit many state museums and archaeological sites, compare the cost of a museum pass with individual tickets.

## Adapting the plan

With a hire car from İzmir, you could add **Aphrodisias** and the white travertine terraces of **Pamukkale** (Hierapolis) before heading to Cappadocia. That adds two days to the trip.
