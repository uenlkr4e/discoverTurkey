This one-way route follows Turkey's south-west coast from Antalya to Fethiye. It combines Roman cities and Lycian tombs with plenty of time on and in the water. It works best with a hire car: fly into Antalya and out of Dalaman, or return the car to Antalya and fly from there.

## Before you go

- **One-way car hire.** Arrange a one-way rental between Antalya and Dalaman in advance, and check the drop-off fee.
- **Choose your season.** Late May to June and September to October have warm seas and fewer crowds. July and August are very hot, and the coast is busy.
- **Plan for the road.** The coast road between Antalya and Kaş is winding and slow, but spectacular. Allow plenty of time and stop for the views.

## With children

Shorter driving days, sandy beaches at Patara and Ölüdeniz, and boat trips with swimming stops make this route suitable for families. Consider replacing the long-distance driving with two bases, one in Kaş and one in Fethiye, and doing day trips from each.
