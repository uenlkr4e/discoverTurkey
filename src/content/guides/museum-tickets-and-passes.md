Most of Turkey's best-known museums and archaeological sites, from Topkapı Palace to Ephesus and the Göreme Open-Air Museum, are run by the **Ministry of Culture and Tourism**. They share one ticketing system, so the same rules and passes apply across the country. Mosques, including Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque, are run separately and aren't part of this system. Palaces such as Dolmabahçe are run by the National Palaces administration.

## Buying tickets

You can buy tickets at each site's ticket office or kiosk, or in advance on the ministry's [official museum site](https://muze.gov.tr/). Buying online lets you skip the ticket queue, which helps at the busiest sites in summer.

Ticket prices for foreign visitors are set centrally and change from time to time, so check the official site rather than relying on figures in older guidebooks or blogs.

## MuseumPass

The ministry sells **MuseumPass** cards for visitors who plan to see a lot:

- **MuseumPass Türkiye** covers state museums and sites across the country.
- **Regional passes** cover **Istanbul**, **Cappadocia**, **the Aegean** and **the Mediterranean**.

A pass is good value if you visit several of the major ticketed sites in a short time, and it can save queuing at the ticket office. It's rarely worth it for a short trip with only one or two museums. Add up the individual tickets for your plan and compare.

The Turkish **Müzekart** is a different card, intended for Turkish citizens and residents. Foreign visitors should look at the MuseumPass instead.

## What passes don't cover

Some special sections need a separate ticket even if you have a pass. The ministry's list currently includes the **Harem at Topkapı Palace**, **Hagia Irene** in Istanbul, the **Terrace Houses at Ephesus** and the **Dark Church** (*Karanlık Kilise*) at the Göreme Open-Air Museum. Check the current list before you buy.

## Free entry

Some visitors get free entry to state museums and sites, including young children and visitors with disabilities. Bring proof of age or disability. The rules for each group are published on the ministry's site.

## Evening openings

In recent summers, some major sites have opened into the evening as part of the [Night Museums](https://goturkiye.com/night-museums) programme. Sites have included Ephesus, Hierapolis (Pamukkale), Patara and Side. Visiting at dusk avoids the worst of the heat. Check which sites are taking part, and their hours, before you go.

## Tips

- **Go early.** The large sites are busiest from mid-morning, especially when cruise ships are in port.
- **Check closing days and seasonal hours.** Summer and winter hours differ, and some museums close one day a week.
- **Keep your ticket or pass handy.** Some sites check tickets again at inner sections.
