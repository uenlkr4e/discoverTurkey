Mobile coverage is good in Turkish cities, towns and along the main roads, and 4G data is fast and widely available. You have three main options: an eSIM, a local SIM card or your home provider's roaming.

## eSIM

If your phone supports eSIM, a travel eSIM is often the simplest option. You buy a data package online before you leave, install it by scanning a QR code, and switch it on when you land. eSIMs usually provide data only, so keep your home SIM active for calls and texts if you need them.

Before you buy, check that your phone is unlocked and eSIM-compatible, and read recent reviews for coverage in the areas you're visiting.

## Local SIM cards

The three main mobile operators are **Turkcell**, **Vodafone** and **Türk Telekom**. All sell prepaid tourist packages with data, calls and texts at their own shops, including at major airports. You'll need your passport to register the SIM, and setting it up can take a little while.

### Phone registration (IMEI) rule

Turkey requires phones brought in from abroad to be registered when used with a Turkish SIM card. Unregistered foreign phones can be used with a Turkish SIM for a limited period. After that, the phone is blocked on Turkish networks unless it has been registered, which involves a fee. Tourist SIM packages are designed around this limit, so for a normal holiday this usually isn't a problem. If you're staying longer, check the current rules before you buy a local SIM.

## Roaming

Many providers offer daily or weekly roaming packages for Turkey, but Turkey isn't covered by EU roaming rules, so standard rates can be very high. Check with your provider before you travel, and consider turning off data roaming until you've confirmed the price.

## Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is available in most hotels, cafés and restaurants, and at airports. Speeds vary, especially in rural guesthouses and cave hotels. Avoid logging in to banking or other sensitive accounts on open public networks, or use a reputable VPN.

## Useful apps

Download offline maps for the regions you're visiting before you go, especially for hiking. A translation app with Turkish offline is handy outside tourist areas, along with the apps for any airlines, bus companies and city transport you plan to use.
