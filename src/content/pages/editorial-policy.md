We want every page on Discover Turkey to be accurate, useful and honest about what it doesn't know. This policy explains how we work.

## Research and review

- **Sources.** Our guides draw on official sources (government, museum, transport and tourism authorities), first-hand experience and reputable published references.
- **Review.** Every page is reviewed by a named editor before publication. The review date and reviewer are shown on each page.
- **Facts that change.** We don't present prices, opening hours, fares, visa fees or entry rules as fixed facts, because they change often. Where they matter, we describe the general picture and link to the official source.
- **Updates.** We re-review published pages at least once a year, and sooner when we learn of a significant change.

## Photographs

We use our own photographs and images published under open licences that allow commercial use, such as Creative Commons licences on Wikimedia Commons. Every image is credited with its author, source and licence beneath it.

## Advertising and independence

The site is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers have no influence over our content, and we don't accept payment for coverage. Ads are labelled "Advertisement" and are visually separated from editorial content. If we ever publish sponsored content or use affiliate links, we will label them clearly.

## Accessibility

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Every image has descriptive alternative text, and the site can be used with a keyboard and screen reader. If you have trouble using any part of the site, please let us know.
