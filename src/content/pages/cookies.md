Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a similar way. This page explains which ones are used on Discover Turkey and how to control them.

## Strictly necessary

The site itself doesn't set cookies to function. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set security cookies to protect the site against bots and abuse. These can't be switched off through our consent settings.

## Consent

Google's consent message stores your choices so it doesn't ask you again on every page. You can change these choices at any time using the **Privacy settings** link at the bottom of every page.

## Advertising

Google AdSense and its partners use cookies to serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. Where consent is required, cookies used to personalise ads are only set after you agree. Learn more in Google's advertising policies at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>.

## Analytics

Google Analytics uses cookies, typically named `_ga` and `_ga_<ID>`, to distinguish visitors and sessions, so we can understand how the site is used. These cookies are only set after you consent. Without consent, Google Analytics receives limited signals that don't use cookies.

## Controlling cookies

You can change your consent choices with the **Privacy settings** link at the bottom of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work.

For more on how we handle personal data, see our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
